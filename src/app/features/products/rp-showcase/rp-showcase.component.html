Built for how India rents

Made for the way you already work

📲UPI, straight to you

Add your UPI ID or QR once. Tenants tap Pay and their own UPI app opens. The money never passes through us.

🤝Free for tenants

Tenants join through your invite link at no cost, so getting them on board is easy.

🧾Receipts tenants can use

Approved receipts stay in the tenant's receipt vault, ready when they need proof of rent.

🔒A closed circle

Only you and your tenant see your tenancy. Brokers browsing listings can't reach your number.

💬WhatsApp, only when you want it

Send invites on WhatsApp. Rent alerts are off by default, and you choose which tenants get them.

🇮🇳Made in Goa

A DPIIT-recognised startup, incubated at CIBA Goa.

01

How we started.

RentPhoenix Technologies began with a clear and specific observation: rental property management is genuinely hard, and no existing software made it meaningfully easier. Landlords kept records in spreadsheets. Tenants communicated over WhatsApp. Agreements lived in physical files. The coordination gap was obvious.

We built RentPhoenix OS to solve it — a rental management platform that consolidates tenants, agreements, payments and maintenance into one system. That product became the foundation of a company.

02

Where we're going.

The company has expanded beyond a single product into a software studio that takes on enterprise software, government technology and automation projects for organisations across India. We are incubated at CIBA Goa and recognised under DPIIT Startup India.

Our focus is building software that genuinely solves coordination problems — for businesses, for government, for organisations that need something real.
